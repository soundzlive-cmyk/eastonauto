FAQ
Do I need multiple estimates?

Not usually. Most insurers accept one estimate from the shop you choose and will send an appraiser if needed. Ask us and we'll walk you through it.

What if more damage is found during repair?

It's common. We document the hidden damage and submit a supplement to your insurer, so you're not left covering it.

Can you help if the other driver was at fault?

Yes. We can work with the other party's insurer directly on your behalf.

Do you help with rental cars?

We can help coordinate a rental through your policy or the at-fault party's insurer.
